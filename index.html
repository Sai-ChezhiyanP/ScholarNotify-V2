WORKSPACE REFERENCE

What Makes Each Tab Different?

A quick guide to the primary question answered by each platform section

OVERVIEW
“What is happening?”

High-level institutional status, school-wide risk and overall progress.

ACTION CENTER
“What needs my attention?”

Urgent students, pending follow-ups and day-to-day intervention actions.

EARLY WARNING
“Who is at risk?”

Search, filter and identify students showing elevated disengagement risk.

MULTI-VECTOR
“Why are they at risk?”

Decompose root causes across attendance, academic, economic, health and behavior.

CASES
“What action is being managed?”

Track formal intervention plans, status transitions, assigned teachers and notes.

FOLLOW-UPS
“What needs to happen next?”

Scheduled check-ins, arrival verifications and post-intervention evaluations.

SMART INTAKE
“Is the student data up to date?”

Ingest new spreadsheets or camera register photos without manual double-entry.

SCHEMES
“What external support is available?”

Match students with state welfare, transport passes, kits and DBT scholarships.

SIMULATOR
“What could different support plans look like?”

Explore projected risk reduction trajectories for varied intervention bundles.

SATHI
“Can peer support help?”

Assign trusted senior buddies for mentoring, check-ins and peer motivation.

SOS / EMERGENCY
“Does this require urgent field response?”

Coordinate home visits, community trusted liaisons and critical caution notices.
